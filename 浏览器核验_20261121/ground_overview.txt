Advice and Clarifications
You will want to switch environments to get different terrain types and to capture water images. You will want to scatter buildings and houses to train for the building class. You will want to add groups of trees to bare areas.
Lush bushes register as trees, only very small and dry bushes are "other".
If you run out of space, download the data by clicking on the 3 dots next to the folder you want to download.
When you are finished, you cannot save your work on cloud, that is a paid Pro user feature. However, you can do the following:
Download Your Edited Scenario
Download Your Edited Scenario
At the end of your session, your Scenario will not save. You can download your entire Scenario file and all of your data, then you can upload that file on your next session.
This should be simple and quick to do, so reach out if you're having trouble.
Next session, click on the 3 dots next to the Scenarios folder, and select "upload folder". Select your previous Scenario folder
